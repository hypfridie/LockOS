# LockOS Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

LockOS ("LockOS," "we," "us," or "our") is an offline Android application designed to protect selected applications on your device using your device's existing security mechanisms.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LockOS accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

LockOS is designed to operate locally on your device. We do not operate a server for collecting or storing your LockOS activity.

Depending on the features you use, LockOS may access the following information locally on your device:

**Installed application information**
- Application name
- Application package name
- Application icon or other basic application metadata
- Whether an application has been selected for protection

This information is used only to display your applications and provide app-lock functionality.

**App activity and protection events**

LockOS may process app-related events locally to determine when a protected application is opened, left, or needs to be protected again. If activity history is enabled, related events may be stored locally on your device so that you can view them in the Activity section.

**Authentication**

LockOS uses Android's system authentication mechanisms where supported, including the device credential and biometric authentication.

LockOS does **not** receive, store, or know your device PIN, password, pattern, fingerprint data, or face-recognition data. Authentication is handled by Android and the device's secure authentication framework.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

LockOS does not intentionally collect or transmit:

- Your device PIN, password, or pattern
- Fingerprint or face biometric data
- Photos, videos, documents, contacts, messages, or other personal files
- Passwords or credentials stored in other applications
- The contents of protected applications
- Your browsing history
- Precise location information
- Personal account information, unless a future feature explicitly requires it and this Privacy Policy is updated accordingly

---

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed by LockOS is used only to:

- Display installed applications
- Allow you to select applications for protection
- Detect when protected applications require locking
- Apply the app-lock functionality
- Provide optional local activity history
- Maintain the application's settings and configuration
- Improve the reliability and security of LockOS

LockOS does not sell, rent, or share your locally processed application information with advertisers or other third parties.

---

## 4. Data Storage

LockOS is designed with a local-first approach.

Settings, protected-app selections, and optional activity history are stored locally on your device when required for the application's functionality.

LockOS does not upload this information to a LockOS server.

If you uninstall LockOS or clear its application data, locally stored LockOS data may be deleted by Android.

---

## 5. Device Credentials and Biometrics

LockOS does not create or manage a separate LockOS PIN or password.

When authentication is required, LockOS relies on the device's existing Android authentication mechanisms, where supported.

Your device credential and biometric information remain under Android's security framework. LockOS does not have access to the underlying PIN, password, pattern, fingerprint template, or facial biometric data.

---

## 6. Permissions and Special Access

LockOS may request Android permissions or special access that are necessary to provide app-lock functionality.

The exact permissions requested may vary by Android version and the features enabled by you.

Where Android provides a system-controlled permission or special access screen, you can review and revoke the relevant access through Android Settings, subject to Android's system restrictions.

LockOS uses requested access only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and within the functionality presented to you.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

The core LockOS app-lock functionality is designed to operate without transmitting your protected-app information to third-party servers.

If a future version of LockOS integrates a third-party service that collects personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe that service and the information it processes.

Google Play and Android may independently process information according to Google's own policies and your device or account settings.

---

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by LockOS.

Because LockOS is designed to process its app-lock information locally, your protected-app selections and local activity data are not intentionally transmitted to a LockOS server.

However, no software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

LockOS is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through LockOS.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

---

## 10. Your Choices and Controls

You can control LockOS through the application and Android system settings, including:

- Which applications are protected
- Whether optional activity history is enabled
- Biometric authentication settings
- Device authentication settings
- Permissions and special access granted to LockOS
- Local application data, including by clearing LockOS data or uninstalling the application

---

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LockOS changes or when required by applicable laws or Google Play requirements.

The updated policy will be published with a new effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
